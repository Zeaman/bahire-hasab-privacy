# Privacy Policy — Bahire-Hasab

**Bahire-Hasab by YomTech, 2018/2026**  
Version 1.2.0 · Effective date: 2026-08-09

## Summary

Bahire-Hasab is an Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church fasting and feast calculator. **Calendar calculations run on your device.** The app does **not** require an account and does **not** include ads.

## Data on your device

Stored only on your phone (not on Bahire-Hasab / YomTech servers):

- Theme preference
- Favorites, year history, and display options
- Optional feast/fast alarm settings (time, sound, vibrate, muted days)

## Network & third parties

- **Feast/fast explanations:** The Play Store production build uses **on-device saved explanations** by default. It does **not** send feast/fast names or dates to Google Gemini unless a future update explicitly enables that feature and discloses it.
- **Share / export:** PDF, DOCX, copy, print, and email use the system share sheet or apps you choose. We do not receive those shares.
- **Add to calendar (optional):** With your permission, feast/fast days can be written to your device calendar via the system calendar APIs.
- **Notifications / alarms (optional):** Local device notifications only; we do not operate a push notification server for this app.

## Permissions (Android)

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Optional morning feast/fast alarms |
| Exact alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) | Reliable alarm timing when you enable alarms |
| Boot completed | Reschedule alarms after device restart |
| Vibrate | Optional alarm vibration |
| Calendar read/write | Optional “add day to calendar” |
| Storage (API ≤28 only) | Saving exports on older Android; API 29+ uses MediaStore Downloads |

The production build does **not** request camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

## Data we collect on our servers

We do **not** run an account system and do **not** collect, store on our servers, or sell personal data.

## Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. It does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s personal data.
